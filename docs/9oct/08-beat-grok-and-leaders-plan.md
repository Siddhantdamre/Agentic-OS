## 0. Summary

**Goal.** For an owner of a 5–200 person business, DareXAI One should be the
obvious pick over Grok (app, Business, Grok Bot), ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and
the AI-employee startups — not because it is a smarter chatbot, but because it
is the only product that *runs the business's operations*, safely, with one
memory, on the channels the business's customers already use.

**Three moves.**

1. **Double down on what nobody else combines** (section 3): prove the
   channels and actions live, make the approval ladder the best governance UX
   in the market, make the Company Brain visible and editable, and let owners
   tune the proactive operations.
2. **Close the table-stakes gaps that make Darex lose the first five minutes**
   (section 4): file and image understanding in chat, voice, cited research and
   deep research, routines from chat, model routing, artifacts with share
   links, a mobile surface, and an owner channel on WhatsApp. Then, second
   wave: browser use, a voice receptionist, image generation, MCP in and out.
3. **Win distribution and trust** (sections 5–6): one public price, proof
   assets, a security page and SOC 2 path, and riding the frontier assistants
   (Darex as an MCP app inside ChatGPT, Claude and Grok) instead of fighting
   them.

**What "better than Grok" means, concretely** (section 1): on the 45-row
matrix in `07` §5, move Darex from 24 ✅ to at least 36 ✅ (target 38) within 90 days,
with every ✅ backed by a live proof, while keeping a lead in governance,
business actions and customer channels that Grok Bot cannot match without
rebuilding its architecture.

## 2. Principles for this plan

1. **Every new capability goes through the existing spine.** Chat request →
   `classify.ts` → employee routing → tool gate → audit → Temporal where
   durable. No side doors that skip approvals or tenancy (`AGENTS.md` §3).
2. **The chat is the front door.** Each feature must be reachable from Ask AI
   and listed in `gap26sept/chat-coverage.md` (generated by
   `services/workflows/src/scripts/chat-coverage.ts`).
3. **Honest by default.** A missing key, model or connector returns a clear
   "not available" with a fix link, never a fake answer (`AGENTS.md` rule 4).
4. **Cost visible.** Every new action has a credit cost shown before it runs
   when the cost is non-trivial (research, voice minutes, image generation,
   browser sessions).
5. **One concern per commit**, migration with its code, `BUILD_STATE.md`
   updated in the same commit (`CLAUDE.md` §2, §5 of this repo).
6. **Env-driven config.** New providers (speech, image, browser) are behind
   env keys with fail-fast in production.

## 3. Differentiators to double down on

These are the areas where `07` §7.1 shows Darex already ahead. The work here
is mostly proof, UX and polish — cheaper than new features and more
defensible.

### 3.1 Make what exists provably live (P0)

**Why.** Every one of the 154 chat-coverage rows is 🔴 "not proved live".
WhatsApp real-number, Instagram, Gmail reply, Outlook and Slack were built and
unit-tested but not run against real accounts (`BUILD_STATE.md` 2026-10-05 to
2026-10-09). A competitor with fewer features that demonstrably works will win
the demo.

**Spec sketch.**
- Pick the **Top 30** tools by SMB value: Gmail (fetch, read, reply, draft),
  Google Calendar (list, create, move), Sheets (read, append), Drive (list),
  HubSpot (contact, deal create/update), Stripe (customer lookup, refund,
  payment link), Shopify (order lookup, address change), WhatsApp send/list,
  Instagram list/send, Outlook list/reply, Slack read/post/reply, web_search,
  web_extract, daily brief, knowledge search.
- For each: a scripted live probe in `infra/scripts/` (pattern:
  `check-phase*.js`) that runs the chat request end to end against a test
  tenant with real sandbox accounts, records the tool call, and flips the
  `chat-coverage.md` status cell to 🟢 by hand with the date and evidence link
  (the generator preserves hand-set cells).
- A **public demo tenant**: one WhatsApp number answering from a seeded
  Company Brain (a fictional real-estate brokerage), with a refund-style
  approval visible on a shared screen.
- A nightly smoke run of the Top 30 in CI against sandbox accounts (Stripe
  test mode, a Google test workspace, HubSpot developer account, Shopify
  development store), failing loudly when a provider changes.

**Files.** `infra/scripts/` (new `check-chat-live.js`),
`docs/new-109-pg-plan/gap26sept/chat-coverage.md`,
`services/workflows/src/scripts/chat-coverage.ts` (optional: add an evidence
column), `.github/workflows/` (nightly job), `BUILD_STATE.md`.

**Human blockers (🔴).** OAuth client IDs for HubSpot, Stripe, Notion, Slack,
Shopify, Zendesk, Intercom, Microsoft, Salesforce; `NEXT_PUBLIC_NANGO_PUBLIC_KEY`;
a real Meta WhatsApp number and Meta token rotation; sandbox accounts.

**Effort.** M (2–3 engineer-weeks plus provider registration time).
**Priority.** P0. Nothing else in this plan matters to a buyer until the core
works on camera.

### 3.2 The best approval and governance UX in the market (P0/P1)

**Why.** Approvals are Darex's sharpest edge against Grok Bot (approvals
advisory, actions logged as the user), ChatGPT and Claude (permission prompts
only). Relevance AI, n8n and Sierra set the UX bar (`07` §4.9, §4.16, §4.19).
Darex has the mechanics (`tool-gate.ts`, `autonomy.ts`, confirm classes,
`agent_pending_actions`, `decide-approval.ts`, Slack interactive approvals via
`slack-interact.ts`) but the gap audit lists UI gaps and three overlapping
approval tables (`gap26sept/00-summary.md` P0 #5, #6; P1 cross-tab).

**Spec sketch.**
1. **One approvals inbox** (`/actions`) with: bulk approve/reject, filter by
   employee, risk and amount, "approve and create rule" (like Grok Bot's
   "create rule"), and the plan or conversation that produced the action.
2. **Approve from where the owner is.** Slack (exists), **WhatsApp reply**
   ("Reply 1 to approve the $84 refund to Priya, 2 to reject") through the
   owner channel (§4.9), web push (`lib/web-push-notify.ts`) and email.
   Approval tokens are single-use, bound to the pending action id and the
   approver's user id, and expire.
3. **Plain-English policy rules** per employee, compiled to the existing
   deterministic checks: "Refunds over $100 need me", "Never message a
   customer between 9 PM and 8 AM" (maps to `quiet-hours.ts`), "Always ask
   before emailing anyone at @bigclient.com". A small LLM call proposes the
   structured rule; the owner confirms it; enforcement is deterministic.
4. **Cost pause.** A turn or plan that would exceed N credits pauses for
   approval (uses `services/workflows/src/cost/budget.ts`).
5. **Independent reviewer for high-risk actions** (Sierra "supervisor",
   Grok "Auto Review" pattern): before a send/pay/delete executes at autonomy
   tier ≥3, a second, cheaper model checks the action against the policy rules
   and the conversation; disagreement downgrades the action to "needs
   approval". Logged as an audit event.
6. **Dry run** for plans: "simulate" executes read steps and renders the
   write steps as previews without calling providers.

**Files.** `NEW-FRONTEND/src/app/(app)/actions/page.tsx`,
`NEW-FRONTEND/src/components/actions/*`, `apps/dashboard/app/api/decisions`,
`apps/dashboard/lib/decide-approval.ts`, `lib/slack-interact.ts`,
`lib/inbound-confirm.ts`, `services/workflows/src/tool-gate.ts`,
`services/workflows/src/autonomy.ts`, `services/workflows/src/quiet-hours.ts`,
`services/workflows/src/cost/budget.ts`, new
`services/workflows/src/guardrails/policy-rules.ts` and
`guardrails/reviewer.ts`, migration `211_policy_rules.sql` (table
`employee_policy_rules`, RLS) — ask first, it is a schema change, and align with
the "one canonical approval table" decision.

**Effort.** L overall: inbox + bulk + rule-from-approval M (2 weeks);
WhatsApp approval reply S (after §4.9); policy rules M (2 weeks); reviewer
model S–M (1 week); dry run M (1–2 weeks).
**Priority.** Inbox and remote approval P0; rules and reviewer P1; dry run P2.

### 3.4 Own the customer front line, and measure it like Fin does (P1)

**Why.** WhatsApp, Instagram and widget answering is the strongest wedge

### 4.1 Real-time web answers with citations, and deep research

**Why.** Research is the most common "wow" task in every frontier assistant
and in Manus/Genspark. Darex's Research and Growth employees are judged on it.
`payment-plans.md` already prices a "Deep Web Research Task" at 20 credits.

**Competitor bar.** Grok DeepSearch (plus real-time X), ChatGPT Deep Research
and Work, Claude Research, Gemini Deep Research (browses hundreds of sites),
Manus Wide Research (parallel sub-agents). All produce cited reports.

**Darex today.** `web_search` (providers in
`services/workflows/src/tools/search-providers.ts`, SearXNG in
`infra/searxng`) and `web_extract` (Crawl4AI) return snippets and page text;
the model writes an answer with no standard citation format or UI.

**Spec sketch.**
- *Level 1 — cited answers (P0).* Tool results from `web_search` and
  `web_extract` carry `{id, title, url, snippet, fetched_at}`. The system prompt
  (`prompts/system-prompt-builder.ts`) requires `[n]` markers for any web-based
  claim. The chat stream emits a `sources` event; the frontend renders source
  chips and a sources drawer. Memory hits get the same treatment (§3.3).
- *Level 2 — deep research (P1).* New `DeepResearchWorkflow` (Temporal):
  1. plan: the model writes 3–8 sub-questions and a report outline (shown to the
     user as a plan card, editable — reuse the plan-confirm UI);
  2. fan out: one activity per sub-question runs search → pick top N →
     extract → summarise with citations, in parallel (cap per org, e.g. 4);
  3. gap check: one round of follow-up queries for unanswered parts;
  4. synthesise: a report with an executive summary, sections, a table where
     useful, and numbered references;
  5. deliver: as a chat artifact (§4.7), optionally saved to the Company Brain
     with employee scope, and optionally to Google Docs.
  Budget: max pages fetched, max tokens, max wall time; cost shown before
  start; progress events streamed ("Reading 14 of 30 sources").
- *Business-aware research.* Before the web, query the Company Brain and
  connected CRM (e.g. "research Acme" first pulls the HubSpot company and past
  emails). This is the angle Grok and ChatGPT cannot copy without the
  business's systems.
- *Scheduled research* comes free with routines (§4.4): "every Monday,
  research what our top 5 competitors shipped".
- *Safety.* All fetched content is untrusted (`services/workflows/src/untrusted.ts`);
  outbound URLs go through `tools/safe-url.ts` (SSRF checks); per-domain rate
  limits; robots.txt respected by Crawl4AI config.

**Files.** `services/workflows/src/tools/web-search.ts`, `web-extract.ts`,
`search-providers.ts`, `tool-definitions.ts` (new `deep_research` tool),
`workflows/DeepResearchWorkflow.ts` (new), `workflows/index.ts`,
`activities/` (new research activities), `prompts/system-prompt-builder.ts`,
`apps/dashboard/app/api/ask-ai/route.ts` (sources event),
`NEW-FRONTEND/src/components/chat/*` (source chips, research progress),
`docs/new-109-pg-plan/gap26sept/chat-coverage.md` (regenerate).

**Effort.** Level 1: S. Level 2: M–L (3–4 weeks). **Priority.** L1 P0, L2 P1.

**Risks.** Search provider quality and rate limits (SearXNG engines get
blocked); add a paid fallback (env-keyed) behind `search-providers.ts`. Cost
blow-up — hard budget caps.

**Acceptance.** "Research the top 5 property-management software vendors in
Pune with pricing, and cite sources" produces a report with ≥10 distinct cited
URLs in under 5 minutes, citations resolve, cost matches the estimate ±20%.

### 4.2 File and image understanding in chat

**Why.** The first thing most owners try. Darex's attach button validates a
file and shows its name but never sends it (`NEW-FRONTEND/src/components/ui/chat-bar.tsx`:
`onSend(text.trim())`). This is a visible broken promise.

**Competitor bar.** Every frontier assistant and agent product: PDFs, images,
spreadsheets, audio.

**Darex today.** Knowledge files can be uploaded in settings and ingested
(`components/settings/knowledge-files.tsx` → ingestion jobs →
`IngestWorkflow`/`EmbedWorkflow`). WhatsApp images and voice notes are already
described or transcribed by a multimodal model (`apps/dashboard/lib/whatsapp-media.ts`).

**Spec sketch.**
- Upload endpoint `POST /api/ask-ai/attachments` (multipart): size cap (e.g.
  20 MB), MIME sniffing (not extension trust), allowed types PDF, DOCX, TXT,
  CSV, XLSX, PNG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC; virus/zip-bomb guard; stored in org-scoped
  object storage with RLS-protected metadata row (`chat_attachments`, migration
  `212_chat_attachments.sql`); signed short-lived URLs only.
- Processing: images → passed to a vision-capable model as image parts;
  PDFs/DOCX → text extraction (+ page images for scanned PDFs → vision OCR);
  CSV/XLSX → parsed to a table preview and made available to the sandbox tool
  for analysis (`tools/sandbox.ts`) ("chart my sales by month from this
  sheet").
- The message carries `attachment_ids`; the agent turn gets a compact summary
  plus a tool `read_attachment(id, pages?)` for large files instead of stuffing
  everything into context.
- "Save to Company Brain" action on any attachment → existing ingestion path
  with employee scope selection.
- Retention: chat attachments deleted with the conversation and covered by DSR
  export/delete (`services/workflows/src/dsr`).

**Files.** `NEW-FRONTEND/src/components/ui/chat-bar.tsx`,
`NEW-FRONTEND/src/app/(app)/chat/page.tsx`, `NEW-FRONTEND/src/lib/validators.ts`,
new `apps/dashboard/app/api/ask-ai/attachments/route.ts`,
`apps/dashboard/app/api/ask-ai/route.ts`, `lib/whatsapp-media.ts` (extract the
shared describe/transcribe helpers into a `lib/media-understanding.ts`),
`services/workflows/src/tool-definitions.ts` (`read_attachment`),
`tools/file-ops.ts`, `tools/sandbox.ts`, `local-turn.ts` / `agent-client.ts`
(multimodal message parts), `infra/litellm/config.yaml` (vision-capable
route), `infra/db/migrations/212_chat_attachments.sql`, `services/workflows/src/dsr/*`.

**Effort.** M (2–3 weeks). **Priority.** P0.

**Risks.** Prompt injection inside documents — treat extracted text as
untrusted (`untrusted.ts`) and never let document text change tool allowlists;
storage cost; PII in uploads (DSR coverage).

**Acceptance.** Upload a 10-page PDF invoice, ask "what's due and when, and
add a reminder" → correct amounts with page citations, and a calendar or task
creation plan card. Upload a photo of a handwritten note → text extracted.
Upload a CSV → sandbox chart artifact.

### 4.3 Voice: dictation, spoken replies, live voice, and a phone receptionist

**Why.** Owners talk to their phones. Grok, ChatGPT and Gemini Live set the
in-app bar; 11x Julian, Fin Voice, Decagon, Sierra and Relevance show that a
voice agent answering the business's phone is worth tens of thousands a year
(`07` §4.12). Darex's mic is a mock (`chat-bar.tsx` inserts a fixed sample
string).

**Spec sketch — three levels.**
- *Level 1: dictation and read-aloud (P0).* Real mic capture with
  `MediaRecorder` → `POST /api/ask-ai/transcribe` → speech-to-text (provider
  via env; Gemini audio input already used in `whatsapp-media.ts`, or a
  dedicated STT API) → text in the composer for review. Optional "read reply
  aloud" via a TTS provider, streamed. Language auto-detect (Hindi, English,
  and the top markets).
- *Level 2: hands-free voice conversation in the app (P1).* A realtime voice
  session (WebRTC to a realtime speech model provider, or STT → agent → TTS
  pipeline with streaming) that uses the same employee routing and tool gate.
  Risky actions still produce an approval card on screen; voice can say
  "I've prepared the refund, tap approve".
- *Level 3: AI receptionist on a phone number (P2, pilot in 90 days).* Twilio
  (tool exists: `tools/twilio.ts`) voice webhook → media stream → realtime
  voice agent bound to the Customer employee with a phone-specific prompt;
  capabilities: answer FAQs from the Company Brain, book/move appointments
  (calendar tools), take a message, transfer to a human number, send a
  WhatsApp/SMS follow-up. Every call: transcript, summary, outcome, logged as a
  conversation (`channel_key = 'voice'`). Consent and disclosure ("you're
  speaking with an AI assistant") configurable per jurisdiction. Billing per
  minute in credits.

**Files.** `NEW-FRONTEND/src/components/ui/chat-bar.tsx` (real mic),
`NEW-FRONTEND/src/components/icons/mic-icon.tsx`, new
`apps/dashboard/app/api/ask-ai/transcribe/route.ts` and `.../speak/route.ts`,
new `apps/dashboard/lib/speech.ts` (provider abstraction, env-keyed),
`lib/whatsapp-media.ts` (reuse transcription), Level 3: new
`apps/dashboard/app/api/webhooks/voice/route.ts`, a small realtime media
service (new `services/voice-gateway/` — the only new service in this plan,
because media streams need a long-lived WebSocket process; justify in
`BUILD_STATE.md`), `lib/channel-normalize.ts`, `lib/inbound-agent.ts`,
`services/workflows/src/tools/twilio.ts`, billing meters.

**Effort.** L1: S. L2: M. L3: L (4–6 weeks to a pilot-quality receptionist).
**Priority.** L1 P0, L2 P1, L3 P2 (start a scoped pilot in weeks 9–13 if a
real-estate or clinic customer commits).

**Risks.** Latency (target < 1 s turn-taking for L2/L3); telephony
regulations (call recording consent, AI disclosure); cost per minute.

**Acceptance.** L1: speak "What did Priya ask on WhatsApp yesterday?" →
correct transcription and answer, on mobile Safari and Chrome. L3: a test
caller books a showing and receives a WhatsApp confirmation; transcript in
the inbox.

### 4.4 Routines: scheduled and event tasks created from chat

**Why.** "Every Monday at 9, send me last week's sales and any refunds over
$100" is the canonical request. ChatGPT (3–15 active tasks by plan), Gemini
Scheduled Actions (10), Grok Bot Routines (50 per Bot), Manus, Genspark
Schedules/Heartbeat, Lindy one-sentence routines, Dust, Zapier and n8n all
have it. Darex only has fixed system sweeps.

**Spec sketch.**
- **Data.** Table `routines` (migration `213_routines.sql`, RLS): `id, org_id,
  owner_user_id, employee_id, title, instruction, schedule (cron + timezone)
  | trigger (event type + filter), delivery (chat thread, WhatsApp owner,
  Slack channel, email), autonomy_cap, credit_cap_per_run, status, created_at`;
  `routine_runs` with the last N runs (status, summary, cost, link to
  conversation/plan).
- **Creation from chat.** New chat tool `create_routine` (and
  `list_routines`, `pause_routine`, `update_routine`, `delete_routine`).
  The model converts natural language into a draft (cron + timezone + delivery)
  and the UI shows a confirmation card ("Every Monday 09:00 IST, Finance
  employee, deliver to WhatsApp — Create / Edit / Cancel"), like Dust's
  natural-language schedule confirmation.
- **Execution.** Temporal Schedules (one per routine, ID
  `routine-<id>`), each run starting an `AutonomousAgentWorkflow` with the
  routine's employee and a `routine_run_id`. Runs obey the employee allowlist
  and autonomy tier, capped by `autonomy_cap`; write actions inside a routine
  default to "needs approval" unless the owner raised the cap. Credit cap per
  run; quiet hours respected.
- **Event triggers.** Phase 2: "when a new lead arrives in HubSpot", "when a
  WhatsApp message mentions 'refund'", "when a Stripe payment fails" — reuse
  inbound webhooks and Nango syncs (`SyncWorkflow`), matched by a filter.
- **UI.** A Routines page (system sweeps from §3.5 and user routines in one
  list), with test run (Grok Bot pattern), pause, history.
- **Limits per plan** to control cost (e.g. Starter 10 active, Growth 50).

**Files.** `infra/db/migrations/213_routines.sql`,
`services/workflows/src/tool-definitions.ts`, new
`services/workflows/src/tools/routines.ts`, `services/workflows/src/workflow-client.ts`
(Temporal schedule create/update/delete), `workflows/AutonomousAgentWorkflow.ts`
(routine context), `periodic-registry.ts` (system routines alignment),
`quiet-hours.ts`, `cost/budget.ts`, new `apps/dashboard/app/api/routines/`
routes, `NEW-FRONTEND/src/app/(app)/` new `routines/page.tsx` (or a tab under
`/plans`), `lib/audit-events.ts` (new kinds `routine.create|update|delete|run`
→ `audit_events_kind_chk` migration), chat-coverage regeneration.

**Effort.** M (3 weeks) for schedules; +M for event triggers.
**Priority.** Schedules P0; event triggers P1.

**Risks.** Runaway cost and spam — caps, minimum interval (1 hour), per-org
active limit; DST/timezone errors — store IANA tz, test DST boundaries.

**Acceptance.** From chat: "Every weekday at 8:30 send me unread customer
WhatsApps and anything waiting on my approval, on WhatsApp" → confirmation
card → routine runs at the right local time, delivered to the owner channel,
visible in history, cost logged.

### 4.6 Multi-model routing, model picker and bring-your-own-key

**Why.** Answer quality on hard tasks is a top reason owners prefer ChatGPT,
Claude, Grok or Gemini. Darex routes everything to one low-cost alias
(`darex-default` → `gemini/gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, fallback Groq
`gpt-oss-120b/20b` in `infra/litellm/config.yaml`). Dust (20+ models),
Relevance (any model + BYOK + fallback) and n8n set the builder bar;
`payment-plans.md` already lists a BYOK add-on at $99/month.

**Spec sketch.**
- **Model aliases by job**, not one default: `darex-fast` (classification,
  routing, short answers), `darex-smart` (planning, research synthesis,
  complex chat), `darex-vision`, `darex-audio`, `darex-reviewer` (§3.2),
  `darex-embed`. Each alias maps to 2+ providers in LiteLLM with fallbacks,
  including at least one frontier model per alias where it matters
  (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI routes are all LiteLLM-supported; choose by
  eval, not brand).
- **Router.** `classify.ts` already decides simple vs complex; extend it to
  pick the alias (complexity, attachments, tools needed, employee). Log the
  choice and cost in Langfuse (already wired).
- **Picker.** Chat composer option: Auto (default) / Fast / Best quality,
  with credit multiplier shown. Admin can restrict to approved models
  (data-residency or provider policy).
- **BYOK.** Org-level provider keys stored encrypted (same pattern as Meta
  tokens), used by LiteLLM via per-request key or per-team virtual keys;
  credits then charge only for actions, not tokens.
- **Evals.** A small eval set per employee in `infra/evals/` (routing,
  tool choice, refusal on not-connected, answer quality) run on any alias
  change.

**Files.** `infra/litellm/config.yaml`, `apps/dashboard/lib/litellm-client.ts`,
`apps/dashboard/lib/classify.ts`, `services/workflows/src/agent-client.ts`,
`local-turn.ts`, `trueforge-client.ts`, `services/workflows/src/cost/track.ts`,
`billing/` (multipliers), `infra/evals/`, settings UI
(`NEW-FRONTEND/src/app/(app)/settings/[tab]`), migration for org model
settings and encrypted keys (`214_org_model_settings.sql`).

**Effort.** M (2–3 weeks) for aliases + router + picker; BYOK +S; evals +S.
**Priority.** Aliases and router P0 (quality), picker and BYOK P1.

**Risks.** Margin: frontier models cost 10–50× flash-lite — the router must
keep most traffic on cheap models and the credit schedule must reflect
"smart" turns. Tool-calling differences between providers — evals catch
regressions.

**Acceptance.** Planning and research turns route to `darex-smart`; simple
lookups stay on `darex-fast`; blended cost per turn within the credit
schedule; eval pass rate not lower than today on every employee.

**Why.** ChatGPT Work, Claude file creation, Gemini Canvas, Genspark

### 4.9 Owner channel: talk to your AI employees on WhatsApp (and Telegram, Slack)

**Why.** Genspark Claw (WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Slack, Signal…), Lindy
(iMessage, Slack) and Dust (Slack, Teams) let the owner work with the agent
from a messaging app. Darex already runs WhatsApp transports and pushes the
daily brief to WhatsApp and Slack — but the owner cannot reply to their
employees there. This is the cheapest way to "be on the phone" in WhatsApp-

**Spec sketch.**
- Map verified owner/staff phone numbers to users (`whatsapp-numbers.ts`

- Inbound WhatsApp from a bound staff number routes to an **internal** Ask AI

  tomorrow"), approvals ("1" / "2" replies to approval prompts, §3.2), voice
  notes (existing transcription), images and PDFs (via §4.2), routine delivery
  (§4.4).
- Risky actions require an explicit confirmation reply plus, above a threshold,
  approval in the app (step-up), because WhatsApp is a weaker auth channel.
- Same pattern for Slack DMs (Slack tools exist) and Telegram (new, P2).

**Files.** `apps/dashboard/lib/whatsapp-inbound.ts`, `lib/inbound-agent.ts`,
`lib/whatsapp-numbers.ts`, `lib/inbound-confirm.ts`,

`216_user_channel_bindings.sql`, `NEW-FRONTEND` settings (bind my phone),
`lib/slack-interact.ts`.

**Effort.** M (2 weeks). **Priority.** P0 (high leverage; reuses most of the
WhatsApp stack).

**Risks.** Number spoofing is not a WhatsApp risk in practice, but account
takeover of the owner's phone is — step-up for payments; rate limits; clear
audit (`channel: whatsapp_owner`).

**Acceptance.** Owner sends a voice note "What's pending for me?" → gets a
list of approvals → replies "approve 1" → refund executes → audit row shows
the owner as approver via WhatsApp.

### 4.10 Browser and computer use for apps with no API

**Why.** Grok Bot's headline: "can use any website". Manus, Genspark, Lindy
(Pro+), ChatGPT (cloud browser), Claude in Chrome and Copilot Studio (preview)
all have it. Darex depends on connectors (`07` L8). SMBs use portals with no
API (property portals, government filings, supplier sites).

**Spec sketch.**
- **Scope it narrowly.** A `browse_task` tool for *read-mostly* jobs first:
  log in with a stored session, navigate, extract a table, download a file.
  Write actions on websites (submit a form, place an order) are always
  approval-gated with a screenshot of the filled form before submit.
- **Runtime.** A sandboxed headless browser per task (container with
  Playwright, or a managed browser provider via env), no access to the
  internal network (egress allowlist; reuse `safe-url.ts` rules), destroyed
  after the run; per-org concurrency cap.
- **Sessions.** The owner signs in once through a live view ("take over" like
  Grok Bot) and the session cookie is stored encrypted per org and per site,
  never shown to the model. Passwords are never typed into chat.
- **Observability.** Step screenshots attached to the run, visible in the
  plan card; full audit.
- **Recording to skill (P2).** "Show me once": record a browser session and
  propose a reusable skill (Grok Bot "watch me once").

**Files.** New `services/browser-runner/` (isolated service; justify in
`BUILD_STATE.md`), `services/workflows/src/tools/browser.ts`,
`tool-definitions.ts`, `tool-gate.ts` (new confirm class `web_submit`),
`activities/` (Temporal activity with heartbeats), `infra/docker-compose.yml`
and `deploy/` (new container, ports inside the Darex range per
`deploy/ports.conf`), encrypted session storage migration
`217_browser_sessions.sql`, chat UI screenshot viewer.

**Effort.** L (4–6 weeks) for read-mostly with sessions; write flows +M.
**Priority.** P2 start (prototype in weeks 10–13 only if §4.1–4.9 are done).

**Risks.** Security is the biggest risk in this plan: prompt injection from
web pages driving actions; session theft; SSRF to internal services; bot
detection and CAPTCHAs; terms-of-service. Mitigations: isolated network, no
shared sessions across orgs, approval on every write, untrusted-content
handling, per-site allowlist chosen by the owner. Flag as security-sensitive
in review.

**Acceptance.** "Download last month's statements from <supplier portal> and
add totals to the Finance sheet" completes with screenshots and one approval,
in a sandbox that cannot reach `127.0.0.1` or the Docker network.
